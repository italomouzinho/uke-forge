## Full description (4000 chars max)

Melodía is a complete practice and performance tool for baritone ukulele players.

**Chord Builder**
Pick a root key and mode (major, minor, or blues) — the app instantly maps the
scale tones and displays fingering diagrams for each chord in your progression.
Choose from preset progressions (i–VI–III–VII, I–IV–V, 12-bar blues) or type
your own Roman numerals.

**Interactive Fretboard Lab**
Tap any combination of frets on the visual fretboard to detect the chord name and
explore harmonic colours. Jump directly from a detected chord to a full
harmonisation preset.

**Fingering Patterns**
Eight strumming and picking patterns with beat-by-beat PIMA notation: Travis pick,
arpeggio, bass drone, pinch pulse, waltz, delta strum, island calypso, and reggae skank.

**Song Builder + Performance Mode**
Assemble named sections into a set list. Tap into full-screen Performance mode —
each chord card fills the screen for easy reading on stage. The screen stays awake
automatically during performance.

**Transpose**
Paste a chord chart (inline or ChordPro format), shift by semitone, and get capo
suggestions for multiple voice ranges. Key-signature-aware spelling ensures Bb
charts stay in flats, not sharps.

**Offline-first**
Works on airplane. No network required after install.
